On restraint in interface design

2026

Essay

Cut before you add. One clear idea beats five small ones.

Every label, animation, and button competes for attention. Remove what you don't need.

Brands that edit hard look confident. Sparse UI looks intentional, not unfinished.

The last pass is the hardest. That's when you delete the stuff you already like.
